Parity Ladder

Five Deus Ex levels, walked one actor at a time. For each level, N is how many actors (trunk order) get built two ways — natively, and by UnrealEd itself — and diffed byte-for-byte. The highest N below is the deepest point verified identical; the ladder stops climbing a level the moment the built packages diverge, and that divergence becomes the next thing to fix.

advancing — verified up to N, next N not yet attempted or still building.
blocked — a real divergence is pinned at N+1; under active investigation, not masked.
