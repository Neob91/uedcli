# DX_LUM story exploration — summary (canon-grounded)

Five writers each developed the existing DX_LUM story bible (`/workspace/dx_lum/DevDocs/Story/`,
distilled in `SEED-BRIEF.md`) into a complete, long story; six analysts (3 opus, 3 sonnet) then read
all five and ranked the strongest twists. This file aggregates the result. Raw material:
`raw-stories/` (5 stories), `findings/` (6 analyses). The earlier off-canon run is in
`archive-pre-bible/` — ignore it.

All five keep canon intact (Ryker; the FMA; Quinmore/U-7134; the Corporate Union; Nuradyne's
universe-siphoning reactors; Atlas; the operative; the stranded liaison; Harmon; Voight; the missing
kid) and honor the player-verb constraints (converse / infolink / kill-incapacitate / multitool
doors / read computers — no surveillance tapes, no timestamp-hacking).

## Consensus top twists (by analyst votes)

Votes = how many of the 6 analysts placed it in their top 3.

1. **"Ryker is the FMA's finished product" — Story E (The Null Suppressor). 6/6 — unanimous.**
   Ryker was salvaged from a universe the FMA let collapse ("let it die, then recruit the wreckage");
   his canon-fixed blank affect is a *maintained neural suppressant*. Praised as the only twist that
   turns the required blank-slate protagonist into the payload, and for being carried entirely by
   readable seeds (a clipboard line, an idle dosing animation, missing backstory options, a stranger's
   recognition) rather than a cutscene — so it rewrites every earlier scene on replay. Highest craft
   marks and strongest player-character impact.

2. **"The best donor is your twin" — Story A (The Meter). 5/6.**
   Congruence physics means the siphon drains near-identical universes, so the drained world has its
   own Quinmore, its own Nuradyne, its own **Kira Voight** — and the hunted "terrorist" operative is
   literally Voight's double. Rated the most original single conceit and the most unforgettable image
   in the set; plays as a face-to-face confrontation the player walks into (choice-contingent, fully
   within the verbs). Extraction-as-colonialism turned on the self.

3. **"The FMA is a licensing cartel" — Story B (The Licensing). 5/6.**
   "Policing inter-universal crime" actually means licensing the same atrocity for member worlds;
   "condemned/assess-and-contain" means *cleared for draining*. The reveal retroactively poisons every
   word of the prologue and recontextualizes Ryker's own "locator" tool as a yield-appraiser. Called
   the cleanest, best-planted version of the essential Deus Ex employer-turn (built from small
   noticeable contradictions, not an exposition dump), and the strongest fit for the canon-invited
   "FMA has a hidden agenda."

Also strongly cited:
- **Story C's liaison twist** (2/6): the FMA's own abandoned man, Coyle (`PRESUMED DECEASED` in the
  prologue file), is the causal root of the genocide — Nuradyne's cover-up literally speaks the FMA
  vocabulary he taught them — and the FMA's "Ledger Protocol" erases the fact rather than the
  suffering. Best-executed Chekhov's-gun setup in the set.

## Recurring twist DNA

- **"The FMA is not what Ryker was told"** (the canon invitation) — every story pays it off, most
  sharply in B (cartel), C (Ledger Protocol cover-up), E (harvest doctrine).
- **"The extraction is colonialism turned on the self"** — A (your twin is the donor), B (member
  worlds drain each other under license), sonnet-2's cross-cut.
- **"Ryker is complicit before the game starts"** — E (he's a product), B (his tool appraises yield),
  C (his agency armed the genocide).

## The strongest single spine (fusion)

The synthesis analyst (`findings/twists-opus-3.md`) and several others converge: **B + A + E fuse
cleanly.** The FMA licenses siphoning (B); the physics means worlds drain their own near-twins (A),
so the FMA's "recruit the wreckage" harvest (E) means Ryker is a licensed world's salvage — the
employer-turn, the cosmic-horror mechanism, and the protagonist reveal become one escalating spine.
Story C's liaison supplies the on-the-ground causal thread that makes it investigable within the
player verbs.
