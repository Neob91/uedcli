# DX_LUM — story exploration seed brief

## What this is

A round of parallel story development for **DX_LUM**, a Deus Ex–like game. Five writers each draft a
complete, long, self-contained story; six analysts then mine all five for the strongest twists.

## Honest note on "based on what I have"

The request was to build a story "based on what I have." **No formal story bible exists in the
repo.** A thorough search turned up none — the codebase is UnrealEd tooling (`uedcli`) plus a Deus Ex
total-conversion pipeline. So "what I have" is reconstructed here from the real substrate the project
is built on. Treat it as anchors and inspiration, not a canon to obey. Diversity across the five
stories is wanted; do not converge.

## The real substrate (anchors, not constraints)

- **Engine / genre.** Built on the Unreal 1 / Deus Ex (2000) engine as a total conversion. So the
  target feel is classic Deus Ex: a near-future conspiracy thriller, immersive-sim structure —
  branching dialogue, multiple approaches (stealth / combat / hacking / social), player choice with
  consequences, morally grey factions, augmented protagonist, hub levels with side missions.
- **"LUM".** In the codebase LUM is the lighting/luminance subsystem (`LUMLightSwitch`, "lumel"
  lighting). Light, shadow, surveillance, and what is seen vs. hidden are a natural thematic motif for
  DX_LUM — optional to lean on, but a strong hook for a Deus Ex–like tone (illumination as knowledge,
  darkness as freedom/blindness, a light-based augmentation or control system).
- **World levels actually in the build pipeline** (real Deus Ex 1 locations — reuse, reskin, or
  ignore as you like): UNATCO HQ, Hong Kong / WanChai Market, an NYC bar / NYC streets. These suggest
  a globe-trotting structure with an institutional HQ, a dense foreign-city hub, and a grounded
  street-level hub.
- **Deus Ex touchstones to riff on (don't copy):** a global conspiracy behind ordinary crises;
  nanotech/mechanical augmentation and its politics; AIs that outgrow their makers; competing secret
  powers (a UN-style security force, a shadow cabal, an insurgency, a plague/pharma angle); a
  protagonist who starts as an instrument of power and discovers what they serve; endings that are
  ideological choices, not just win/lose.

## What each writer must deliver

A **complete, LONG** story document. Not an outline — a fleshed-out narrative. Include:

1. **Logline** (1–2 sentences) and **premise**.
2. **Setting & world** — time, place, the state of the world, the technology, the central tension.
3. **Factions & power structure** — at least 3, with agendas that genuinely conflict.
4. **Protagonist** — who they are, their augmentation/edge, their arc, what they believe at the start
   vs. the end.
5. **Full plot** — beginning to end, act by act or mission by mission. Actually tell the story: what
   happens, in what order, who betrays whom, what is discovered. This is the core; make it long and
   specific.
6. **Key characters** — allies, antagonists, ambiguous figures; give them motives and secrets.
7. **The central twist(s)** — call them out explicitly and land them.
8. **Player-choice structure & endings** — the immersive-sim branches and 2–4 distinct endings that
   are ideological, Deus Ex style.
9. **Themes** — what the story is actually about.

Aim for depth and originality. Make it feel like a game you'd want to play. Distinct voice and
premise from a generic Deus Ex clone.
