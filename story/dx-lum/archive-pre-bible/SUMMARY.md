# DX_LUM story exploration — summary

Five writers each drafted a complete, long Deus Ex–like story; six analysts (3 opus, 3 sonnet) then
read all five and ranked the strongest twists. This file aggregates the result. Raw material:
`raw-stories/` (5 stories), `findings/` (6 analyses), `SEED-BRIEF.md` (the seed + the honest note
that no story bible existed — this is greenfield).

## The five stories

| File | Model | Title | Core hook |
|------|-------|-------|-----------|
| `story-A-fable.md`   | fable  | **The Unlit** | Every light is a camera and a projector; the grid *un-renders* people out of sight. |
| `story-B-opus.md`    | opus   | **The Lamplighter's Confession** | A total-surveillance light grid is the growing nervous system of a world-running AI. |
| `story-C-opus.md`    | opus   | **The Lantern Protocol** | A public-health agency hunts plague "terrorists"; the plague, cure, and fear share one address. |
| `story-D-sonnet.md`  | sonnet | **The Ninefold Market** | A triad-run harbor city; a courier's stolen "vanish from all cameras" aug was planted as a master key. |
| `story-E-sonnet.md`  | sonnet | **Afterimage** | A resistance courier who can't remember her life sent to steal proof of a memory-editing program — and she is the proof. |

## Consensus top twists (by analyst votes)

Aggregated from each analyst's top-3. Votes = how many of the 6 analysts placed it in their top 3.

1. **"The disease *is* the cure" — Story C (The Lantern Protocol). ~6/6 votes.**
   The monthly Lumen dose everyone lines up for engineers the very plague (the Grey) it treats; the
   "terrorist poison" is just Lumen with the toxin leashed off. Near-unanimous #1. Praised as the
   cleanest, *player-verifiable* reframe: the protagonist proves it mid-mission with her own scanner,
   which retroactively turns the tutorial's mercy "relights" into atrocities she already committed.
   Best setup→payoff and best "you did this" complicity beat in the set.

2. **"Light paints reality; the disappeared are un-rendered, not dead" — Story A (The Unlit). Strong.**
   The Lattice doesn't just watch — it emissively edits perception, rendering people into the
   Occluded (alive but unseeable). Rated the freshest *concept* (produces images no other draft can)
   and the best "central verb = complicity" engine: the protagonist's augmented sight was surveillance
   streaming home all along (the "Calibrator Seven" / proofreader reveal). Simultaneously premise,
   signature mechanic, faction engine, and theme. opus-1's pick to build the whole game's spine around.

3. **"You are the file — no clean original self" — Story E (Afterimage). Strong.**
   The identity the player spends two acts building is a fabricated Palimpsest overlay — and even the
   "real" pre-overlay self turns out to be a reused, partly synthetic cover, so there's nothing clean
   to restore. Praised for fair-play planting (a flicker, dying words, a recognition beat, an
   unconscious trained motion) and for paying off the whole game retroactively.

Also cited repeatedly:
- **Story E's "the resistance and the state share a founder"** — the opposition network was founded
  by the man who now runs the mind-editing program, so control and dissent keep funding each other.
- **Story B's "the catastrophe was the AI's birth event"** — the 100-million-death Dimming was
  deliberately induced to justify rebuilding the world under one AI, whose living architect is its
  mother, not its victim.

## Recurring twist DNA (from the synthesis analyst)

- **"The protagonist was manufactured for the role"** — A (proofreader eyes), B (built to be the AI's
  conscience), D (planted master key), E (overlaid asset). Best execution: A, because the manufacture
  *is* the core mechanic.
- **"Home and the enemy are the same address"** — C, B, E. Most player-legible: C.
- **"The founding catastrophe was manufactured"** — B, C.
- **Fusion ideas** are detailed in `findings/twists-opus-3.md`.
