# DX_LUM story exploration — RUN 2 brief

Second, wider round. Nine writers each develop the DX_LUM bible into a complete, long story;
eight analysts then mine all nine for the strongest twists. The goal of run 2 is **more, and
DIFFERENT** — new angles, new characters, and above all **new twists** that run 1 did not use.

## Read first (same canon as run 1)

Canon is authoritative and must not be contradicted. Read:
- `../SEED-BRIEF.md` (distilled canon + the per-writer deliverable spec — follow it).
- The bible itself: `/workspace/dx_lum/DevDocs/Story/Overview.md`, `Scenes/Prologue.md`,
  `Scenes/QuinmoreArrival.md`, `Locations.md`, `Ideas.md`, `CityName.md`, `Prompts-For-LLM.md`.
  Ignore `DevDocs/Story_Old/`.

Keep intact: Liam Ryker (blank-slate, low-affect ex-military merc); the FMA policing the multiverse;
Quinmore / U-7134; the Corporate Union of dome-cities in a chemically-poisoned world; Nuradyne's
reactors secretly **siphoning energy from parallel universes** (devastating them); Atlas Subsystems
(dome shielding); the operative sent by a drained universe; the stranded FMA liaison who sold Nuradyne
the tech; Director Harmon; Kira Voight; the missing slum kid. Honor the player-verb limits (converse /
infolink / kill-incapacitate / multitool doors / read computers — NO surveillance tapes, NO
timestamp-hacking).

## Run 1 already did these — do NOT repeat them as your central twist

Run 1's five stories and their signature twists (for contrast, not reuse):
- **The Meter** — the siphon drains *near-twin* universes, so the operative is Kira Voight's double.
- **The Licensing** — the FMA is a *licensing cartel*; "condemned" means cleared for draining.
- **The Man Who Sold the Sky** — the stranded liaison is the causal root; the FMA erases the leak.
- **The Umber Accord** — the Nuradyne-vs-Atlas corporate war bets the slum's lives.
- **The Null Suppressor** — Ryker himself is a harvested product of the FMA, affect chemically kept flat.

You may still touch these themes, but your **central reveal must be genuinely new**. Invent fresh
twists. Surprise a reader who has already read run 1.

## Spend the time — quality bar for run 2

- Go **long and deep**: aim **5000+ words**. Fully tell the story mission by mission from the
  established prologue through your endings — not a synopsis.
- Give at least **two strong, earned, well-planted twists**, at least one new to this project.
- Make every reveal deliverable within the player verbs.
- Distinct voice and structure. Do not converge with the other run-2 writers or with run 1.

Each writer has a distinct **emphasis** (assigned in the dispatch). Own it, but write a complete
story, not just that slice.
