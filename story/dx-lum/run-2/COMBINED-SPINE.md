# DX_LUM — combined spine: "The Caretaker" (fusing run-2 stories 07 + 09 + 01)

A single proposed backbone that grafts the three top-voted run-2 twists into one game. It keeps the
canon bible intact and every reveal inside the player verbs (converse / infolink / kill-incapacitate /
multitool doors / read computers). This is a spine, not a script — the three source stories
(`raw-stories/story-07-opus-rogue.md`, `-09-fable-cosmology.md`, `-01-opus-androids.md`) hold the
scene-level detail.

## Why these three fuse

They share the canon skeleton (prologue → outpost → Northgate/Aire Gardens → cordon → datacube →
Harmon → holding-cell kid → liaison → operative → act-3 turn on the FMA) and stack into **three layers
of one reveal**, each answering the previous one's "why":

- **07 The Caretaker Clause = the institutional/personal spine.** Ryker wasn't sent to investigate and
  come home; "investigator" is cover for a one-way *caretaker* posting (`CTKR-7134`). The stranded
  canon liaison is his decommissioned predecessor. And the FMA's seal-and-abandon doctrine is *what
  created* the drained world that blew the reactor — abandonment breeds the monster.
- **09 The First Sky = the cosmic truth underneath.** The reactors don't steal energy; they steal
  *possibility* ("Lume") — drained worlds go deterministic before they go dark. Travel/theft run only
  along "umbilic" scars, so every drained world is U-7134's own **descendant**: U-7134 is the **root**
  of the multiverse. The FMA's inner "Vigil" keeps an 80-year deathwatch ("a quiet root is a safe
  root") and has now armed the **Root Directive** — prune the root, let this world die alone, to spare
  the canopy.
- **01 Good Help = the investigative layer that makes it playable.** The operative hides inside the
  invisible servant-android caste ("the bomber is the help"); those Homestead cores are built from the
  same leaked FMA science as the reactors, so they've been unwittingly **recording the drained world's
  death** in the margins of household footage — a city-wide archive nobody reads. The FMA's live order
  is to **purge** that record and reclaim the tech.

**The single unifying idea:** the FMA's "hidden agenda" is one institution wearing three faces —
abandonment-as-doctrine (07), triage-of-the-root (09), and erase-the-evidence (01). Not a cartoon
conspiracy: a bureaucracy that prices some lives as externalities and calls the silence containment.
Ryker is simultaneously its *caretaker* (07), standing on the *root world* it's about to prune (09),
while sent to *delete the one record* that would expose it (01).

## The layered reveal (what the player peels, in order)

1. **The crime isn't terrorism** — the reactor is a multiversal siphon (canon), surfaced via the
   datacube, Harmon's suppressed readings, the "not a bomber, a walker that doesn't breathe" hunt.
2. **The victim is our own descendant, and the payload is possibility** (09) — the drained world is a
   daughter of U-7134; the reactors burn Lume. Landed through the servant-core archive (01) and the
   dying operative's testimony.
3. **The FMA has watched for years and sent Ryker to clean up** (01) — the "faint anomaly" was the
   siphon *breaching*, not news; the reclamation/purge order goes live.
4. **Ryker is the caretaker; the liaison is his predecessor; sealing made the monster** (07) — read
   off the outpost mission file: `CARETAKER, SUBSTRATE U-7134 — INDEFINITE. Repatriation: N/A`, dated
   before the prologue.
5. **U-7134 is the root, and the Vigil has armed the Root Directive** (09) — the deepest layer: the
   caretaker doctrine exists because this world's stock anchors every other; the FMA would rather prune
   the root than lose the canopy. Ryker's own partner is holding the arming codes.

Each layer recolors the last. The act-3 infiltration is Ryker's *own* outpost (shared by 07 and 09).

## Act spine

- **Act 1 — the investigation they wrote for him.** Canon prologue, with planted burrs: the greyed
  objective (`RECLAMATION` in 01 / `CTKR-7134` in 07), a medical "scar-field baseline" flag (09), a
  partner whose infolink runs warm and personal in a way that's wrong for a short op. Northgate teaches
  the habit of not-seeing the servant caste (01). Cordon, datacube, Harmon stonewalls, the kid hands
  over "a walker that doesn't breathe / the old man in the undercity called it."
- **Act 2 — the book on Quinmore.** The servant-core archive reveals the drained world's death (01);
  the liaison (07/09/01) gives the siphon truth and the first word of "Caretaker"; the operative — a
  synthetic pilgrim hiding as help — gives the drained world firsthand and the direction "the pipe
  goes *down*" (09). Voight's purge of the Custodians is the vise: she's right about the target and
  destroying the only record to get it (01). Midpoint betrayal: Ryker reports up; the FMA's answer is
  "maintain position, you are the resident officer, the book is closed" — the portal collapses (07).
- **Act 3 — the root, and what he does about it.** Infiltrate the FMA's own outpost/Vigil archive
  (07+09): the Root Directive, the root cosmology, the caretaker roster with the liaison's name above
  Ryker's. Build the endgame from whoever he turned — Voight, Harmon, Atlas, the slum crew, the
  operative, the liaison, the partner — and choose at the reactor aperture.

## Endings (four ideological, unified)

The three drafts' endings collapse into one fork answered at the aperture:

1. **The Caretaker / Reclamation (order).** Accept the posting or execute the FMA's cleanup — purge the
   record, secure the tech, let the daughter world die unwitnessed. The system wins by being
   survivable; Ryker becomes the liaison, completed. (07-A / 01-A)
2. **The Pruning (triage).** Side with the Vigil's arithmetic: collapse the root's scars, spare the
   canopy, let Quinmore go dark sector by sector. The most defensible and most monstrous math. (09-1)
3. **The Gift / Return Pump (sacrifice) or The Seal (sever).** Invert the siphon to feed the root's
   descendants back their possibility — Quinmore dims permanently to keep the tree lit (09-2) — or
   collapse every aperture to save the drained world at the cost of the domes (01-C). Two flavors of
   "pay with your own city."
4. **The Waking Root / Publish (truth).** Broadcast the whole archive down every living scar and onto
   every CU channel at once — un-eraseable because it lives in ten thousand servant machines. You
   cannot quietly prune a root that thirteen universes are now watching. Nothing solved, everything
   known. (09-3 / 01-B)

(A fifth, pure-Ryker option survives: **keep everything as leverage and decide later** — 01-D / the
cynical "new liaison" — if you want a dark-neutral route.)

## What this spine buys

- One coherent, escalating mystery where every layer reframes the last, and the FMA reveal pays off
  three ways instead of once.
- The cosmic idea (09) stays grounded because it's uncovered through 01's concrete, within-the-verbs
  investigation (a servant's logs, a child's overheard line, a console) and anchored by 07's personal
  mirror (Ryker = the liaison = a whole world).
- Every established canon beat and character keeps its place; nothing is retconned, only deepened.
